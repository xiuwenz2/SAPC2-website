## Call for Papers

Teams participating in SAPC2 have the opportunity — but are not required — to submit a
workshop paper describing their system, to be presented at the **SAPC2 competition workshop at
NeurIPS 2026** in Sydney, Australia. The SAPC2 session is tentatively scheduled for
**Friday, December 11, 2026, 15:30–17:45 (Room MR C4.3)**, subject to change.
The workshop paper is **non-archival**: submitting here does not prevent teams from later
submitting the same or an extended version of the work to other venues (e.g., Interspeech, ICASSP).

### Submission Requirements
- **Length:** 3–6 pages, excluding references.
- **Format:** No specific template is required; we recommend the <a href="https://media.neurips.cc/Conferences/NeurIPS2026/Formatting_Instructions_For_NeurIPS_2026.zip">NeurIPS style template</a>.
- **Submission site:** TO BE ANNOUNCED (OpenReview link will be posted here).
- **Review process:** Lightweight. Papers will **not** be rejected based on technical content —
  review checks only that a paper is relevant to the competition and clearly written enough to
  understand.
- **Anonymity:** Single-blind (author names visible to reviewers).
- **Archival status:** Non-archival. Submitting a paper here does not prevent later submission
  of the same or extended work to other venues (e.g., Interspeech, ICASSP).

### Eligibility
Submitting a workshop paper is **optional**. It is not required to compete in the challenge, to
be eligible for the prize pool, or to attend the workshop — it is offered as an opportunity for
teams to present their work to the workshop audience. Teams who wish to present at the workshop
should still confirm their intent to attend by the deadline above. Papers should describe the
submitted system(s), including methodology and any additional data used.
